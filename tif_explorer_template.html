How to read these figures

Annual tax extraction and spending describe flows during a year. Ending balance is a snapshot, and cumulative extraction includes earlier years. Adding balances or cumulative values across years double-counts time. A larger balance does not establish available, uncommitted funds, and a larger amount does not measure community benefit.

The checks identify negative stored values and differences between annual extraction and the change in cumulative extraction. These are prompts to inspect reports, not automatic corrections. Unflagged values have not all been independently verified. Different year coverage may reflect reporting, district lifespans, or missing records.
