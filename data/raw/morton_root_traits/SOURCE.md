## Affiliation and interpretation

Morton is listed as a research facility and author affiliation. This is a separate Morton-affiliated research dataset, not the original MCDC street-tree inventory. The notebook uses six numerical root traits and keeps identifiers and group labels outside PCA fitting.

The archive dictionary and methods disagree about diameter and specific-root-length units. Values are preserved as archived. Consistent unit multipliers cancel after standardization, but the discrepancy must be resolved before physical-unit interpretation. This teaching analysis does not reproduce the entire paper.
